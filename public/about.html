Your account

Reading, listening and practice are open to everyone. If you sign in with Google, we save only what keeps your place:

  • Your name and email, from Google
  • The last page you read
  • Your bookmarks
  • Practice scores for each ayah (never your voice recordings)
  • Reciter, theme, text size and translation language
